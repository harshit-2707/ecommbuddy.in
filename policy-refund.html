Refund & Cancellation Policy

At **EcommBuddy.in**, we provide time-based and expertise-based services. Due to the nature of digital agency work, our refund policy is as follows:

1. Website Development & Design

  • Deposits: The initial 50% deposit required to book your slot is non-refundable once the project has started (i.e., once research, wireframing, or design work has commenced).
  • Completed Work: No refunds will be issued for completed milestones or fully delivered websites once approved by the client.

2. Marketing Services (Meta Ads, PR, Management)

  • Service Fees: Monthly management fees are non-refundable once the billing cycle has started. You are paying for our time, setup, and expertise, not a guaranteed outcome.
  • Ad Spend: Money paid directly to third-party platforms (Facebook, Google, etc.) for advertising budgets is never refundable by EcommBuddy.in, as we do not hold these funds.

3. Cancellation Protocols

  • Retainer Services: Clients may cancel ongoing marketing/management services with a **30-day written notice** via email to harshit@ecommbuddy.in before the next billing cycle.
  • Mid-Project Cancellation: Cancellation of a development project midway by the client requires payment for all work completed up to that date on an hourly basis, exceeding the deposit if applicable.
