Solving Payment Issues

In India people who sell things online had a problem. This problem was about getting paid and sending out the things they sold. Indian sellers would ask things like "How do I get paid using UPI?". What about Cash, on Delivery how does that work for people who buy from me?"

Shopify has done a job with this. If you want to use Razorpay for online payments or set up rules for paying cash on delivery Shopify has made it simple. For example you can add a fee when someone pays cash on delivery to stop them from returning things. At EcommBuddy we think it is very important to set these things up from the start. This helps to reduce losses when people return things, which's a big problem, for many Indian merchants who sell things online. EcommBuddy wants to help Indian merchants reduce these losses by setting up Shopify from Day 1.
