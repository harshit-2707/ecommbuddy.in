Shopify Store Development in India

Published: January 16, 2026 • Category: Strategy

If you have a business in India you know things are different now. You cannot just have a shop on a street and expect people to come in. These days your customers are looking at things, on Instagram Reels they are finding products. Buying them on impulse even at 11 PM.

To get their attention you need to have a store that is really good. Your online store needs to be great it needs to be fast and work well so you can catch your customers when they are looking for things to buy online.

1. Why Shopify?

Shopify is really helpful. We work with Shopify a lot making Shopify stores, for people. We have seen what Shopify can do. Shopify is not a place to show your products. Shopify is a way to make a system that sells things for you even when you are not working, like when you're sleeping. Shopify does this for you.

2. Solving Payment Issues

In India people who sell things online had a problem. This problem was about getting paid and sending out the things they sold. Indian sellers would ask things like "How do I get paid using UPI?" or "What about Cash on Delivery?"

Shopify has done a job with this. If you want to use Razorpay for online payments or set up rules for paying cash on delivery Shopify has made it simple. At EcommBuddy we think it is very important to set these things up from the start.

3. Speed is Money

Speed is money. This is a fact: if your website takes longer than 3 seconds to load on a mobile phone you will lose the customer. We work on making images smaller, arranging products in a way, and using simple themes so your store loads very fast even in smaller cities.
