If you want people to remember your store you need to make it look different, from the "Dawn" theme stores. Custom development lets you do your thing and not follow the rules. We can make things like:

  • Custom Product Pages: These are made for your product. For example a jewelry store needs a setup, than a protein supplement store. This is because a jewelry store and a protein supplement store are very different and need to show their products in ways. Custom Product Pages help with this by giving you a layout that is tailored to your product like a jewelry store or a protein supplement store.

  • Wholesale Portals: A locked section for your B2B clients to order in bulk.

3. Speed & Performance

Google has made it very clear that Core Web Vitals are really important.

If your website shifts around while it is loading, which is called Core Web Vitals or it takes too long to become interactive Google will rank your website lower.

This is because Google wants to make sure that people have an experience when they visit Core Web Vitals websites.

So if your website has a lot of Core Web Vitals problems like a CLS or a high LCP Google will not rank it as high, as other websites.

Standard themes are usually too much because they want to do everything for every person. When we do custom development we can get rid of the code that we do not need and make the critical rendering path better. The result is that the website feels fast even when you are on a 4G network in a smaller city like a Tier-2 city. Custom development is great because it helps us make a website that's super quick and that is what custom development is all about making custom development work, for the website and the people who use the website.
